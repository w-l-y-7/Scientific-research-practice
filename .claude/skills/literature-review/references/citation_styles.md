# 引用格式参考

本文档详解文献综述中常用的几种学术引用格式。

> 下面所有示例用的都是**真实存在、且已核对过书目信息**的经济学文献。照着格式仿写时，
> 把作者、年份、卷期页码换成你自己的即可。

## APA 格式（第 7 版）

### 期刊论文

**格式**：Author, A. A., Author, B. B., & Author, C. C. (Year). Title of article. *Title of Periodical*, *volume*(issue), page range. https://doi.org/xx.xxx/yyyy

**示例**：Acemoglu, D., Johnson, S., & Robinson, J. A. (2001). The colonial origins of comparative development: An empirical investigation. *American Economic Review*, *91*(5), 1369-1401. https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.91.5.1369

### 图书

**格式**：Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of work: Capital letter also for subtitle*. Publisher Name. https://doi.org/xxxx

**示例**：Angrist, J. D., & Pischke, J.-S. (2009). *Mostly harmless econometrics: An empiricist's companion*. Princeton University Press.

### 图书章节

**格式**：Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of chapter. In E. E. Editor & F. F. Editor (Eds.), *Title of book* (pp. xx-xx). Publisher.

**示例**：Card, D. (1999). The causal effect of education on earnings. In O. Ashenfelter & D. Card (Eds.), *Handbook of labor economics* (Vol. 3A, pp. 1801-1863). Elsevier. https://doi.org/10.1016/S1573-4463(99)03011-4

### 预印本

**格式**：Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of preprint. *Repository Name*. https://doi.org/xxxx

**示例**：Barucci, E., Gurgone, A., Iori, G., & Azzone, M. (2025). Central bank digital currency, flight-to-quality, and bank-runs in an agent-based model. *arXiv*. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21071

### 工作论文

**格式**：Author, A. A. (Year). Title of working paper (Working Paper No. xxxxx). National Bureau of Economic Research. https://doi.org/xx.xxx/yyyy

经济学的会议论文大多以工作论文形式流传，正式会议论文集的引用格式与图书章节相同。

## Nature 格式

### 期刊论文

**格式**：Author, A. A., Author, B. B. & Author, C. C. Title of article. *J. Name* **volume**, page range (year).

**示例**：Acemoglu, D., Johnson, S. & Robinson, J. A. The colonial origins of comparative development: an empirical investigation. *Am. Econ. Rev.* **91**, 1369-1401 (2001).

### 图书

**格式**：Author, A. A. & Author, B. B. *Book Title* (Publisher, Year).

**示例**：Angrist, J. D. & Pischke, J.-S. *Mostly Harmless Econometrics: An Empiricist's Companion* (Princeton University Press, 2009).

### 多作者

- 1-2 位作者：全部列出
- 3 位及以上：列出第一作者后加 "et al."

**示例**：Barucci, E. et al. Central bank digital currency, flight-to-quality, and bank-runs in an agent-based model. *arXiv* https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21071 (2025).

## Chicago 格式（作者-年份）

### 期刊论文

**格式**：Author, First Name Middle Initial. Year. "Article Title." *Journal Title* volume, no. issue (Month): page range. https://doi.org/xxxx.

**示例**：Acemoglu, Daron, Simon Johnson, and James A. Robinson. 2001. "The Colonial Origins of Comparative Development: An Empirical Investigation." *American Economic Review* 91, no. 5 (December): 1369-1401. https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.91.5.1369.

### 图书

**格式**：Author, First Name Middle Initial. Year. *Book Title: Subtitle*. Edition. Place: Publisher.

**示例**：Angrist, Joshua D., and Jörn-Steffen Pischke. 2009. *Mostly Harmless Econometrics: An Empiricist's Companion*. Princeton: Princeton University Press.

---

## Vancouver 格式（编号制）

### 期刊论文

**格式**：Author AA, Author BB, Author CC. Title of article. Abbreviated Journal Name. Year;volume(issue):page range.

**示例**：Acemoglu D, Johnson S, Robinson JA. The colonial origins of comparative development: an empirical investigation. Am Econ Rev. 2001;91(5):1369-401.

### 图书

**格式**：Author AA, Author BB. Title of book. Edition. Place: Publisher; Year.

**示例**：Angrist JD, Pischke JS. Mostly harmless econometrics: an empiricist's companion. Princeton: Princeton University Press; 2009.

### 正文引用

按出现顺序用上标数字："Recent studies^1,2^ have shown..."

---

## IEEE 格式

### 期刊论文

**格式**：[#] A. A. Author, B. B. Author, and C. C. Author, "Title of article," *Abbreviated Journal Name*, vol. x, no. x, pp. xxx-xxx, Month Year.

**示例**：[1] D. Acemoglu, S. Johnson, and J. A. Robinson, "The colonial origins of comparative development: an empirical investigation," *Am. Econ. Rev.*, vol. 91, no. 5, pp. 1369-1401, Dec. 2001.

### 图书

**格式**：[#] A. A. Author, *Title of Book*, xth ed. City, State: Publisher, Year.

**示例**：[2] J. D. Angrist and J.-S. Pischke, *Mostly Harmless Econometrics: An Empiricist's Companion*. Princeton, NJ, USA: Princeton University Press, 2009.

## 期刊名常用缩写

经济学和金融学的期刊缩写没有像医学 NLM 那样的单一权威表，EconLit 和各家期刊自己的用法略有出入。以下是通行写法：

- American Economic Review: Am. Econ. Rev.
- Econometrica: Econometrica
- Journal of Political Economy: J. Polit. Econ.
- Quarterly Journal of Economics: Q. J. Econ.
- Review of Economic Studies: Rev. Econ. Stud.
- Journal of Finance: J. Finance
- Journal of Financial Economics: J. Financ. Econ.
- Review of Financial Studies: Rev. Financ. Stud.
- Journal of Monetary Economics: J. Monet. Econ.
- Nature: Nat.
- Science: Science
- PLOS ONE: PLoS ONE

**缩写不是硬要求。** APA 和 Chicago 都要求写全称，只有 Nature、Vancouver、IEEE 才用缩写。选定了就用到底，别一半缩写一半全称。

## DOI 最佳实践

1. **务必核验 DOI**：用本项目的 `citation-management` 技能批量核验，它会产出校验报告；本技能自带的 `verify_citations.py` 只做单点查询，适合零散检查
2. **写成 URL 形式**：https://doi.org/10.xxxx/yyyy（优于 doi:10.xxxx/yyyy）
3. **DOI 后不加句点**：DOI 应是最后一个元素，后面不带标点
4. **确认跳转**：检查 DOI 是否正确解析到目标论文
5. **小心从正文粘来的尾标点**：`10.1257/aer.91.5.1369.` 末尾那个句号不是 DOI 的一部分，会让查询失败

## 正文引用指南

### APA 格式
- (Acemoglu et al., 2001)
- Acemoglu et al. (2001) demonstrated...
- 多条引用并列：(Angrist & Pischke, 2009; Card, 1999; Acemoglu et al., 2001)

### Nature 格式
- 上标数字：Recent studies^1,2^ have shown...
- 或：Recent studies (refs 1,2) have shown...

### Chicago 格式
- (Acemoglu, Johnson, and Robinson 2001)
- Acemoglu, Johnson, and Robinson (2001) found...

## 参考文献表组织方式

### 按引用格式
- **APA、Chicago**：按第一作者姓氏字母顺序
- **Nature、Vancouver、IEEE**：按正文中首次出现的顺序编号

### 悬挂缩进
多数格式采用悬挂缩进：首行顶格，后续行缩进。

### 一致性
全文保持格式统一：
- 大小写（标题式 vs. 句子式）
- 期刊名缩写
- DOI 呈现方式
- 作者姓名格式
- **经济学文献特别容易出岔子的一处**：同一篇论文的预印本版和期刊版算不算两条？通常引用正式发表版；正式版尚未出来时才引工作论文，并标注其未经同行评审。
